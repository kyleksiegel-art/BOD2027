Round Recap — Variants

Two expanded treatments and a collapsed state — click any card header to collapse or expand it. Collapsed, the recap is a single summary line; expanded, it's the full report. Both variants fold in the improvements: a bump chart / leader ribbon instead of the barcode spark, a one-sentence narrative, and a tighter hierarchy. Data is fabricated (Streamsong Black).
