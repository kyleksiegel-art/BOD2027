Round Recap — Live Story

Not a live view and a separate recap — one card, driven by round state, that changes what it leads with as the round unfolds and settles into the final recap when it's done. Left to right: Opening → Moving day → Closing → Final. Same skeleton (headline + narrative + leader ribbon + leaderboard), re-weighted per act. Hybrid treatment. Fabricated Streamsong Red (Round 1) data.

How it drives itself. The act is derived from data buildRoundDetail already produces (thru, holeResults, projection, status); the card re-renders on every score change through the existing useLiveQuery path (same-device saves and other phones via Realtime). Round 1 has no prior round, so the story stays within the round — no “biggest move” here. The collapse behavior from the earlier mockup layers on top of this.
