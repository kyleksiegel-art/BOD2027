Round Recap

A deterministic, offline-computed report generated the moment a round completes — winner and margin, the round-winner payout, closest-to-pin, the best closing nine, and the biggest move in the overall standings. Same card, two sizes: an in-context block on the round page and a portrait image for the group text. Numbers below are fabricated to show the shape.

Where it lives. The card renders on /rounds/:n above the Leaderboard, and its compact form headlines Home as “what just happened.” The Share button rasterises the card to PNG on-device (no signal needed) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Every fact is derived from data the scoring engine already produces — holeResults, pointsByPlayerHole, buildStandings, ctp_results — so no AI and no network.
